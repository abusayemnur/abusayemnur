Hi, I'm Abu Sayem Nur.

I am a Computer Science & Technology graduate from Kurigram Polytechnic Institute, Bangladesh, with a growing interest in software engineering, Artificial Intelligence, web development, networking, research and technical writing.

My journey in technology is driven by curiosity and the desire to turn ideas into working applications. Rather than limiting myself to academic learning, I explore technologies through personal projects, experimentation, debugging and documentation.

I have worked on projects ranging from AI assistant prototypes and Telegram bots to educational websites and network traffic analysis tools. Each project gives me an opportunity to understand how different technologies work together in real applications.

I am continuing to strengthen my programming fundamentals and software development skills while exploring the possibilities of AI and machine learning. My long-term goal is to grow into a capable engineer who can build useful, reliable and meaningful technology.
